# Winning Demo Script

## 90-Second Story

UrbanFix AI turns citizen reports into prioritized, trackable municipal action.

1. Open the landing page and point out the live system-transparency strip.
2. Select **View Verified Demo** to establish the outcome first: SLA timeline, before/after proof, two-party verification, and explainable priority.
3. Return to **Report Issue**, upload a civic issue image, and show Gemini's category, severity, routing, confidence, and reasoning.
4. Confirm the location, submit, and copy the generated case ID.
5. Open **Authority Portal**, use judge access, and show the new case in the priority queue.
6. Explain that deterministic rules—not AI—set priority and that low-confidence analysis requires human review.
7. Assign a contractor with a budget cap and SLA.
8. Return to public tracking and show the same case ID and accountability trail.

## Judge Questions To Preempt

- This is not just a complaint portal. It is a decision-support platform.
- AI interprets messy citizen input; deterministic scoring prioritizes action.
- MongoDB stores complaint, status, contractor, offer, and proof history.
- Contractor work is controlled by government approval, budget caps, verification, and proof.
- Citizen validation helps prevent fake closure.
- The interface labels live services and demo fallbacks; no simulated integration is presented as production.

## Recovery Plan

If an external AI or database service is slow, select **View Verified Demo** and continue the complete accountability story with clearly labeled seeded records. Do not spend stage time debugging credentials.

## Strong One-Liner

UrbanFix AI turns a citizen's photo and location into prioritized, trackable government action.
